🔤 Spelling Trail

One graduated trail: a Phonics Ladder using real Pokémon Pokopia items (Phase A), then Pokémon-name fluency practice (Phase B). Each round mixes review, current-level, and stretch questions around the frontier.

📖 Reading Trail

Fixed 5-choice formats — Read & Choose (see a picture, pick the word) and Reverse Read & Choose (see a word, pick the picture) — so difficulty stays constant round to round.

🔢 Math Trails

Two graduated tracks. Each round mixes review, current-level, and stretch questions around wherever each track's frontier is.

🖼️ Pokémon Visual Math

Not a separate mode — the same Math Trails questions above, shown as Pokémon icons instead of bare numbers whenever the numbers involved are still small enough to draw. Leads the early levels automatically, then fades on its own as the trail's numbers grow past what pictures can show.
