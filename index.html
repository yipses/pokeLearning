Poké Learning

Every round

He chooses English or Maths on the home screen. A round mixes the trails switched on below for that subject.

%

English

Spelling

Fill in the missing letters of a Pokémon or an item, starting with short, regular words.

Reading

See a picture and pick its word, or see a word and pick its picture.

Alphabet

A run of the alphabet with a gap: pick the Pokémon whose name starts with the missing letter.

Sentences

Build a true sentence about a Pokémon from word tiles — “Pikachu is yellow”.

Riddles

Read and decide: Yes or No, Who am I?, and Fix it (find the wrong word).

Word Pairs

Match pictures to their words, using the words from his Reading level.

Maths

Maths

Adding, taking away, times and sharing, each with a counting-pattern version. A new kind opens when the one before it is far enough along.

Maths Pairs

Match sums to their answers, using the sums from the maths levels he has reached.

Advanced

Every ladder is a file the app reads at start-up, authored in the design spreadsheet: data/spelling_levels.csv, reading_levels.csv, alphabet_levels.csv, sentence_levels.csv, riddle_levels.csv, word_pair_levels.csv, math_tracks.csv, math_levels.csv and math_pair_levels.csv. A level changed above moves where he is on that ladder; it does not change the ladder.
